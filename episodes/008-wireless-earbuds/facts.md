# Facts — What's inside wireless earbuds? (ep. 8)

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| Inside each bud: lithium-ion battery, Bluetooth system-on-chip (radio), dynamic driver (speaker), MEMS microphones | https://avantree.com/blogs/knowledge/what-do-i-need-to-know-about-how-earbuds-work |
| Do not open / puncture earbud batteries: risk of fire | https://digprjsurvey.amazon.com/csad/help/node/GZ2URF9V5Z8YXRUS (Echo Buds safety: "Do not disassemble, open, crush… puncture… attempt to access the batteries… could result in fire") |
| Speaker = voice coil attached to a diaphragm in a magnet's field; current moves the coil, the diaphragm makes sound | https://avantree.com/blogs/knowledge/what-do-i-need-to-know-about-how-earbuds-work |
| Bluetooth Classic (BR/EDR, used for music) hops across 79 channels at up to 1,600 hops per second, 2.4 GHz band | https://scdn.rohde-schwarz.com/ur/pws/dl_downloads/dl_application/application_notes/1c108/1C108_0e_Bluetooth_BR_EDR_AFH.pdf |
| ANC: mics sample outside noise, a chip computes the anti-phase (inverted) wave, played by the driver, destructive interference cancels the noise | https://avantree.com/blogs/knowledge/what-do-i-need-to-know-about-how-earbuds-work ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Active_noise_control |
| Earbud battery often under 50 mAh (AirPods Pro: 44 mAh per bud; typical 20–50 mAh) | https://theapplewiki.com/wiki/AirPods_Pro ; https://seller.alibaba.com/blogs/2026/southeast-asia/consumer-electronics/wireless-earbuds-battery-capacity-guide-alibaba-b2b |
